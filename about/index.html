● MY STORY

I'm a Business Intelligence Analyst and Data Analyst based in Metro Detroit, passionate about transforming raw data into clear, actionable insights. With experience at Fortis and CAMI Energy, I've built end-to-end reporting solutions — from data pipelines and ETL workflows to executive dashboards and KPI reporting — that help teams make smarter decisions.

My toolkit spans Power BI, Amazon QuickSight, SQL, Python, Tableau, and Excel. I love the challenge of taking messy, complex datasets and turning them into something clean, visual, and meaningful. Whether it's a live operational dashboard or a deep-dive ad hoc analysis, I care about making data accessible and impactful.

Outside of work, I'm always exploring new datasets and building portfolio projects — from analyzing NASA meteorite data to surveying the data professional community. I believe the best analysts are curious people, and I bring that curiosity to every project.
