● MY STORY

My path to data analytics wasn't a straight line — and I think that makes it more interesting. For several years, I managed a pet store, leading a team of about 50 people and keeping day-to-day operations running smoothly. But if I'm being honest, the parts of the job I loved most were never the management tasks. They were the moments when I got to dig into spreadsheets, analyze sales trends, forecast inventory, and spot patterns in the numbers. The data work was always the best part.

By 2022, I was burned out and ready for a change. I made the decision to fully pivot into analytics — the thing I'd always been drawn to. I threw myself into learning: completing the Merit America Data Analytics Program, earning my Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate, and teaching myself tools like SQL, Python, Power BI, and Tableau through hands-on projects and a lot of curiosity. That self-teaching phase taught me something important about myself — I genuinely love the process of figuring things out.

That pivot led me to CAMI Energy, where I built and maintained Power BI dashboards tracking KPIs, installation trends, and energy usage. From there I moved into my current role as a Business Intelligence Analyst at Fortis, where I work across data pipelines, executive reporting, QuickSight dashboards, and SQL-driven ad hoc analysis every day.

At the core of it all, I'm a problem solver. I've always been the kind of person who loves puzzles — whether that's a 1,000-piece jigsaw on the coffee table, an escape room with friends, or a messy dataset that needs untangling. Data analytics is just the professional version of that same itch, and I wouldn't have it any other way.
