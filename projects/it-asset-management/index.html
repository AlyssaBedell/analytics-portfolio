THE BUSINESS PROBLEM

Without centralized visibility into asset health, IT departments are forced to react to equipment failures, compliance violations, and surprise costs. The question this dashboard answers: which assets need attention now, and which departments are most at risk?

● HOW IT WORKS

I built this dashboard to track servers, laptops, printers, and other IT assets across departments. The data was cleaned and transformed using Python, removing duplicates, standardizing formats, calculating days until warranty expiration, and assigning risk scores based on lifecycle stage and compliance status.

The cleaned data feeds a Power BI model with a risk matrix at its center, segmenting assets into high, medium, and low risk by department. Slicers for risk level and department allow the asset management team to drill into exactly what needs attention without wading through the full dataset.

Key visuals include a warranty expiration line graph for planning ahead, stacked bar charts showing risk distribution across departments, a lifecycle overview, and maintenance cost breakdowns by asset type.
