● PROJECT OVERVIEW

I developed this IT asset management dashboard to give asset management teams a real-time view of their infrastructure. The dashboard tracks warranty expiration timelines, lifecycle stages, maintenance costs, and risk levels across departments — helping teams prioritize which assets need attention before they become compliance issues.

Data cleaning and preparation was handled using Python, including removing duplicates, standardizing formats, calculating days until warranty expiration, and assigning risk scores. The cleaned data was then loaded into Power BI where I built all the reporting visuals and KPIs.

The risk matrix is the centerpiece of the dashboard — it visually segments assets into high, medium, and low risk categories by department, so teams can immediately see where to focus their efforts. Slicers for risk level and department allow interactive filtering across all visuals.
